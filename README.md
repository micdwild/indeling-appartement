# Indeling appartement

An interactive floor plan of our apartment: a living room with an open kitchen, a bedroom and a balcony, all drawn to scale in centimetres.

- **Plan:** drag, rotate and add furniture, see clashes and free space, save layouts.
- **3D:** look around in an overview or walk through the rooms at eye height, at any time of day or year and in any weather.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site. Everything is one self-contained page; the 3D view loads three.js from a CDN.

Layouts are saved in your own browser. To move a layout to another browser or person, use **Copy current layout** and **Load code** in the Layouts card.
